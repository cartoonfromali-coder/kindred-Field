Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Open Mile values the privacy of its visitors. This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be collected when you use our website and how that information may be handled.

1. Information You Provide

We may receive information that you voluntarily submit while using features of the website, including forms or other interactive elements.

2. Automatically Collected Information

Website systems and service providers may automatically collect limited technical information during a visit.

  • IP address and general geographic information.
  • Browser, device, and operating system details.
  • Pages viewed and interaction information.
  • Referral sources and campaign identifiers.
  • Basic website performance information.

3. Why Information May Be Used

Information may be used to operate Open Mile, improve website content, understand visitor behaviour, measure campaigns, maintain security, and present useful content or promotional opportunities.

4. Third-Party Offers and Links

Open Mile may provide links to third-party promotions, services, advertisements, or CPA offers. Selecting one of these links may take you away from Open Mile and to a website controlled by another party.

Third-party providers may use cookies, tracking technologies, or other information associated with your interaction with their offer. Their own privacy policies govern information collected after you leave Open Mile.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Cookies, pixels, tags, and similar technologies may be used by Open Mile or its service providers for functionality, analytics, campaign measurement, fraud prevention, and advertising purposes.

6. Analytics

We may use analytics services to understand traffic patterns, website performance, content engagement, and campaign activity. Analytics providers may process technical information according to their own policies.

7. Advertising and Performance Measurement

Promotional partners and advertising providers may receive limited information related to visits, clicks, referrals, or offer interactions where their technology is used.

8. Sharing Information

Information may be shared with service providers that assist with hosting, analytics, website functionality, security, advertising measurement, or third-party offers. We do not sell personal information merely because someone visits the website.

9. External Websites

Open Mile is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, content, or policies of external websites. We encourage visitors to review the applicable privacy policy of any third-party website they visit.

10. Data Protection

Appropriate reasonable measures may be used to protect information. However, internet communications and electronic storage cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Managing Your Preferences

Depending on your location and device, you may be able to manage cookies, browser storage, and certain advertising preferences through browser settings or controls provided by third parties.

12. Children

Open Mile does not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been submitted by a child, please use the contact method provided on the website.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised periodically. Any updated version will replace the previous version when published on this page.

14. Contact

Privacy questions can be directed through the contact method provided on the Open Mile website.
