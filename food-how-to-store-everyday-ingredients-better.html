How to Store Everyday Ingredients Better

Good storage can make a kitchen easier to use and help you keep track of what you have. The best approach depends on the ingredient, available space, and how quickly you use it.

Start With the Basics

Before reorganising your kitchen, think about which ingredients are easiest to lose track of. These are often the foods that sit at the back of cupboards, get buried in the fridge, or are difficult to see.

Keep Frequently Used Items Accessible

Ingredients you use regularly should be easy to reach. Keeping them in the most accessible parts of your storage space can make everyday cooking quicker.

  • Keep common cooking ingredients within easy reach.
  • Group similar items together.
  • Avoid hiding frequently used ingredients behind rarely used ones.
  • Make sure containers are easy to identify.

Use Storage That Matches the Ingredient

Different foods have different storage needs. Follow appropriate storage guidance for perishable foods and keep dry ingredients in suitable, clean, sealed containers where appropriate.

Label When It Helps

Labels can be particularly useful for opened ingredients, prepared food, or anything that is difficult to identify at a glance. Clear information can reduce unnecessary checking and help you use things in time.

Rotate Older Ingredients Forward

When adding new items, bring older suitable items towards the front. This simple habit makes it easier to notice what should be used first.

Keep the Fridge Easy to Read

A crowded fridge can make it surprisingly difficult to remember what is available. Leaving some visible space and grouping similar items can make everyday choices easier.

Review Your Storage Regularly

Storage does not need to remain exactly the same. Every few weeks, notice which areas become cluttered and adjust them around the way you actually cook.

Better storage is less about perfect organisation and more about making the food you already have easier to see, reach, and use.
