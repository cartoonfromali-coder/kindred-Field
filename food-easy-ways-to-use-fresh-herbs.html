Easy Ways to Use Fresh Herbs

Fresh herbs can make simple meals feel brighter and more interesting. You do not need complicated recipes to make good use of them; a small amount can change the character of an everyday dish.

Use Herbs as a Finishing Touch

One of the easiest approaches is to add fresh herbs near the end of cooking. This can help preserve their fresh character and gives you an easy way to lift otherwise simple meals.

A small handful can work well over soups, pasta, vegetables, grains, or other dishes that need a little extra freshness.

Add Them to Simple Sauces

Fresh herbs can become part of a quick sauce or dressing. Chopped herbs mixed with ingredients such as oil, citrus, or yoghurt can create an easy addition to many different meals.

Try Them With Vegetables

Roasted, steamed, or pan-cooked vegetables can benefit from fresh herbs. Instead of relying on a complicated seasoning mixture, try one herb that complements the vegetable you are cooking.

  • Parsley can add freshness to savoury dishes.
  • Basil can work well with tomatoes and lighter dishes.
  • Mint can add brightness to vegetables and salads.
  • Coriander can bring a fresh flavour to many strongly seasoned meals.

Make a Quick Herb Butter

Softened butter mixed with chopped herbs can be kept ready for simple meals. A small amount can add flavour to bread, vegetables, grains, or cooked dishes.

Use Stems Where Appropriate

Some herbs have tender stems that can be chopped and used alongside the leaves. This can reduce unnecessary waste and make the most of what you have bought.

Harder stems can still be useful for adding flavour to stocks or cooking liquids before being removed.

Keep Herbs Fresh for Longer

Different herbs respond differently to storage, so check how each variety is best kept. In general, avoiding unnecessary moisture and using herbs while they are fresh can help you get more from them.

Start With Small Amounts

If you are unsure how strongly a herb will affect a dish, begin with a small amount and taste as you go. This makes it easier to find a balance that suits the rest of the meal.

Once you become familiar with a few herbs, they can become an easy everyday tool for making simple food feel a little more considered.

Keep exploring simple ways to make everyday meals more interesting without adding unnecessary complexity.
